# Little Owl

Athena's little companion: a private Twitch and Discord bot for the AthenaUS community.

## Features

- **Silver coins:** earn coins by chatting on Twitch and taking part on Discord, then spend, give, or gamble them
- **Pokémon:** explore to find wild Pokémon, catch them with Poké Balls from the shop, and build your collection
- **Linked accounts:** connect your Discord and Twitch accounts to share one balance across both

For the full command list, visit the [Parthenon website](https://parthenon.app/commands).

## Contributions

Little Owl is built and maintained by Athena for her community. It is not open to outside contributions, and pull requests and issues are not accepted.
